The player writes only five keys; the data meter writes six and this page one theme key — twelve in total. Sinhala fonts and audio clips live in the service-worker cache, not in localStorage.

Offline behaviour

  • The shell, the fonts and the 30-word session are pre-cached, so the app opens without a network.
  • A published session appears on your device the next time you open the app while online.
  • Dictionary audio is cached after the first play in its own cache and replayed for zero data.
