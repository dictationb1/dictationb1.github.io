How this was measured — so you can check it yourself

  1. Open the app in Chrome, then DevTools → Network, and tick Disable cache.
  2. Press Start dictation → I’m ready and finish all 30 words.
  3. Read the total at the bottom of the Network panel — you will see about 0.82 MB (Lite) or 2.28 MB (with video).
  4. Repeat the same lesson: the total drops to a few kilobytes, because every clip is pinned in the cache.

The app also shows this live on the phone: the 📊 pill at the bottom-left (and 📊 Data Sheet in the top bar) displays the exact number of bytes downloaded in that session, counted by wrapping window.fetch.
